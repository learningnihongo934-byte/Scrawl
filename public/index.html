    How to play

    Draw to cast

    Your six runes sit along the bottom. Draw one anywhere on the book and pause for a moment. If the page recognizes it, it becomes the spell. If not, it fizzles. Or press 1 to 6 (or tap a rune) to quick cast at 70% strength.

    Where and how you draw

    Shots (Bolt, Scatter, Beam, Orb, Familiar) fly at your rival from where you drew them. Guards (Wall, Mirror, Mine, Mire, Vortex) go where you draw them on your half. Lobs (Sigil, Comet, Rain) land anywhere, even right on your rival. Curses (Hex, Mark, Silence, Leech) hit your rival wherever you draw them. Self spells (Shield, Thorns, Mend, Haste, Cleanse, Echo) work on you.

    Bigger drawings make stronger spells but cost more ink. Neater drawings are stronger too.

    Standing your ground

    Wizards never move. Block with walls, shoot spells down with your own, or shield up.

    Fusions

    Draw a rune on top of one of your own walls, sigils or orbs to fuse them: fire on storm makes Plasma, frost on earth makes Glacier, and so on. A bolt drawn on your own wall launches the whole wall as a Ram.

    Flourish

    Cast again within 1.6 seconds and the next spell grows stronger, up to ×1.4.

    Elements

    Eleven elements, each strong against two others and each with a status: Fire burns, Frost chills, Storm shocks, Venom poisons, Gale winds, Radiant dazzles, Umbral hexes, Iron shatters shields, Life drains, Earth is heavy and Void slips through walls. Your runebook shows who beats whom.

    Winning

    Best of three rounds, 90 seconds each. Ink (mana) refills over time, and each rune needs a moment to dry before you can cast it again.
